InstaCollage is an open-source, production-grade Progressive Web App (PWA) and backend API that unpacks all individual photos from public Instagram carousels and arranges them into studio-grade photo collages, polaroid moodboards, and cinematic filmstrips in one click using hardware-accelerated HTML5 Canvas.

InstaCollage automatically calculates the median dominant natural aspect ratio of the incoming slides and scales the overall canvas and tile coordinate matrix accordingly. This eliminates vertical cutoffs and letterboxing.
